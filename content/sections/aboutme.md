I'm a PhD Candidate in the [Department of Economics](https://economics.ucdavis.edu/) at the University of California, Davis. I also work as a Research Data Specialist at the State of California's Employment Development Department. Starting in October I will be a Postdoctoral Fellow with the [California Policy Lab](https://www.capolicylab.org/) at UCLA.

My primary research interests are related to the design of social insurance and safety net programs. I am also interested in the economics of health behaviors. My recent work on the US Unemployment Insurance program has been funded by the Washington Center for Equitable Growth and the Bilinski Educational Foundation.
